Profit = product sell price − material purchase cost. Products use the 24-hour flea average (or the best trader buy price if not flea-tradable); materials use the cheaper of the flea average and the trader price. Tools marked materials are returned, so they are excluded from the cost. Fuel cost, flea fees (raised since 1.1.0) and selling time are not included, so actual profit is lower than shown. Prices swing a lot even within a day, so treat this as a reference only.
