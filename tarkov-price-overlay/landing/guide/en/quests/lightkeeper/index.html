All 15 quests · Kappa required 0· Data as of 2026-11-04

<Classified>

Since 1.1.0, most side quests unlock in groups of 2~4 per trader loyalty level (LL). Completing a few quests in the same group or raising the trader LL opens the next group; only some still chain one after another like before. The table below is sorted by LL → group → chain depth → level to roughly match the "order you will receive them".
