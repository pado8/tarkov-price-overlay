Jaeger Quest order Jaeger

All 64 quests · Kappa required 4· Data as of 2026-11-02

Before the conflict, he worked as a hunter in the Priozersk Natural Reserve under the State Hunting Service. A professional hunter and survival specialist. Even now, he still guards the reserve's hunting grounds from various aggressive individuals.

Since 1.1.0, most side quests unlock in groups of 2~4 per trader loyalty level (LL). Completing a few quests in the same group or raising the trader LL opens the next group; only some still chain one after another like before.
