Peacekeeper Quest order Peacekeeper

All 51 quests · Kappa required 0 · Data as of 2026-11-04

UN peacekeeping Force supply officer, based in one of the central checkpoints leading to the Tarkov port zone. The blue helmets have been seen poking their heads into small deals from the very beginning of the conflict, buying everything of value in exchange for western weapons, ammo, and all kinds of military equipment.

Since 1.1.0, most side quests unlock in groups of 2~4 per trader loyalty level (LL). Completing a few quests in the same group or raising the trader LL opens the next group; only some still chain one after another like before. The table below is sorted by LL → group → chain depth → level to roughly match the "order you will receive them".
