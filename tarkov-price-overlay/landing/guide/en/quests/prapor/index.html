Prapor Quest order Prapor

All 66 quests · Kappa required 1· Data as of 2026-11-04

The Warrant officer in charge of supply warehouses on the sustaining base enforcing the Norvinsk region blockade. Secretly supplied the BEAR PMC operators with weapons, ammunition, and various other provisions he had at his disposal during the Contract Wars.

Since 1.1.0, most side quests unlock in groups of 2~4 per trader loyalty level (LL). Completing a few quests in the same group or raising the trader LL opens the next group; only some still chain one after another like before.
