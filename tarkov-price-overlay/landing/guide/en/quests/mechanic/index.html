Mechanic Quest order Mechanic

All 72 quests · Kappa required 1 · Data as of 2026-11-04

A former chemical plant foreman, who from the very beginning of the conflict took to weapon modification, repairs, and maintenance of complex equipment and technology. He prefers clandestine solo living and operates discreetly, while placing complicated and challenging tasks above all else.

Since 1.1.0, most side quests unlock in groups of 2~4 per trader loyalty level (LL). Completing a few quests in the same group or raising the trader LL opens the next group; only some still chain one after another like before. The table below is sorted by LL → group → chain depth → level to roughly match the "order you will receive them".
