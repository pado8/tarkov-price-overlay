Prestige Requirements Overview Prestige

Requirements and rewards per level for PVE·PVP · Data as of 2026-11-03

Prestige is a system where a high-level character who meets the requirements resets their progress and starts over in exchange for unique rewards. Since 1.2.0 (2026-10-06), PVE also has 6 levels, and PVE level 1 has lighter requirements than PVP. On reset you keep some gear and part of your skills and weapon mastery (carry-over rate by level: PVE 10~50%, PVP 5~30% — per the official wiki Prestige page), and you can move allowed item types up to the "Carry-over slots" size. PVE Prestige resets only the PVE character.

Prestige rewards by level (PVP and PVE: shared and different)

Every level: a level-specific dogtag, armband, achievement and profile icon (shown next to your nickname), plus +1 Charisma skill level. The skill and weapon mastery carry-over percentage and the transfer grid grow with each level, and PVE additionally grants TarCoins. Prestige levels and rewards persist through profile resets.

Prestige FAQ

What do you keep and what do you lose when you prestige?
You keep profile statistics, achievements, Arena ARP rating and armory progress, the listed percentage of skills and weapon mastery, and the items placed in the transfer grid. Level, quest progress, trader reputation, hideout and the rest of your stash are reset.
How do PVE and PVP prestige differ?
The conditions differ. PVE level 1 needs only level 15 and 5,000,000 roubles; PVP level 1 needs level 25, the quest New Beginning, three hideout modules (Intelligence Center 1, Nutrition Unit 2, Rest Space 2) and 10,000,000 roubles. From level 2 both require completing the Tour and skill levels. For rewards, the PVE carry-over percentage is nearly double, and TarCoins are PVE-only. PVE prestige resets only the PVE character.
Where do I check the conditions?
The PVE and PVP tables above are the full conditions from public data. All six must be met: level, quests, story chapter objectives, skills, hideout and roubles on hand. From level 5, completing Collector (Kappa) is also required.
