Icebreaker Icebreaker

Map info · Bosses 4 · Extracts 1 · Related quests 21 · Data as of 2026-11-04

In the Gulf of Finland, trapped within the blockade surrounding Tarkov, lies the nuclear-powered icebreaker "Boreas", owned by the logistics corporation Paradigm Shipping. The exact purpose of "Boreas" and the cargo it carries remain unknown. It appears that Paradigm Shipping and TerraGroup made special efforts to minimize any mention of the icebreaker in the press and keep its routes and assignments classified.

Walkthrough notes

  • At first you can only enter by transit from the piers on Lighthouse or Shoreline, and you must first unlock this map in the story chapter Boreas. Taking the transit requires a Sudak-Tudak marine repair kit.
  • After you finish the Stick to It questline you can enter directly from the map selection screen. The marine repair kit is still required.
  • Insured items left on this map are never returned.
  • The Wedge spawns in the gym on level 3 with 3 to 6 guards, and Knight on level 0 with 2 Rogue guards. The number of Black Division operatives and Wedge guards depends on how many players are in the raid.
  • The helicopter is the only PMC extract. Fire a green flare into the sky inside the flare zone; if it does not climb high enough, the helicopter will not come.

Checked against the official wiki guide section (2026-11-04). If a patch changed this, let us know via feedback.
