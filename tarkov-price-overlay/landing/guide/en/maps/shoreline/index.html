Shoreline Shoreline

Map info · Bosses 5 · Extracts 19 · Related quests 81 · Data as of 2026-11-04

The Shoreline area makes up a large part of the outskirts of Tarkov and is located next to the Port. The area houses a partially abandoned village, modern private housing, agricultural fields, long stretches of beach, a boating facility, gas station, weather station, and a cellular tower as well as other commercial facilities. Shoreline's main point of interest is the Azure Coast health resort which consists of several blocks, exclusively powered by a nearby hydroelectric power plant. The resort had previously been used as temporary accommodation for members of TerraGroup and the corporation's associated divisions in preparation for the evacuation of the Tarkov Port.
