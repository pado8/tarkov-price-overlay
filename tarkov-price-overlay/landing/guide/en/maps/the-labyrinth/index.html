The Labyrinth The Labyrinth

Map info · Bosses 1 · Extracts 2 · Related quests 9 · Data as of 2026-11-02

A facility of one of TerraGroup's contractors, Knossos LLC. According to public sources, they build amusement and theme parks. However, this place looks more like a heavily fortified bunker than a new theme park.
