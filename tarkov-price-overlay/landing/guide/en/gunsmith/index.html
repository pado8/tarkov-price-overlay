How to use — type the names from the "full parts checklist"below into the Flea Market search and press ★ (favorite) so they all appear together when you check prices. In the table, "Required parts"means the part must be in the build; other things like ergonomics·recoil·weight under Stat requirements can be met with any parts. Expand "View wiki example build" on each row to see the example build posted by the official wiki (parts·trader·LL); those parts are also in the checklist under the "Example" column. You hand over the whole assembled weapon, so if two quests need the same part you need two of it (count column). Owned checks are saved only in this browser.
