Tarkov New Player Guide From the Tour to level 10

For new characters and right after Prestige · Game 1.3.0.1 · Data as of 2026-11-04

Just these three things first
  1. When you start, the story quest line Tour is assigned automatically. Push through it in order to unlock Skier → Mechanic → Prapor → Peacekeeper and the maps one by one.
  2. Side quests open after a trader is unlocked, in batches of 2~4 per loyalty level (LL). Complete a few or raise the LL and the next batch appears.
  3. For Tour hand-ins, keeping 5 FiR building materials/tools and 2 FiR weapons plus money (250,000₽ · 8,000$) ready in advance means you will not get stuck.

3. How side quests unlock (since 1.1.0)

  • For each trader, the LL1 base quests open first; complete a few or raise the LL and the next batch (2~4) opens. The 'Group unlock'label on quest pages refers to this.
  • Some still form chains as before, with a big reward at the end such as a rare purchase or craft unlock (the reward is shown when you accept the first quest).
  • Scav kill objectives count only regular and sniper Scavs and player Scavs. Bosses, Raiders and Rogues do not count.
  • Some objectives can now be completed by group members (icon on the quest screen). Some quests are still solo-only.
  • Loyalty level requirements no longer include sales volume — only level and reputation remain.

4. Special trader unlocks

JaegerIntroduction (Mechanic) — given when you talk to Mechanic. Find the encrypted message at the Jaeger camp on Woods and hand it in to unlock Jaeger. Jaeger matters for food/medical quests and the Hideout, so do it as soon as Mechanic opens.
RefEasy Money - Part 1 (PVP) / PVE: Easy Money - Part 1 (PVE) (Skier) — Level 10 + Skier LL2. Put up the poster in the living room of the Scav base on Customs to unlock Ref.
LightkeeperReworked in 1.1.5, the 'To the Light' chain — requires Lighthouse unlocked via the Tour, plus LL3 with every trader except Fence and Ref + Fence reputation 2.0 + asking Mechanic about Lightkeeper to start. (The old 'level 33 · Network Provider' requirement from older guides no longer applies)

7. Early tips

  • The Found in Raid (FiR) mark only applies to items you picked up in a raid and brought out alive. Gear returned via insurance and items bought from traders or the flea do not have it.
  • Since 1.1.0, trader sell prices are up about 25% on average, trader buy prices are down 20%, and the flea market fee rose from 3% to 5%. Where you sell makes a bigger difference now.
  • Using the Tour rule that '3 visits'·'dogtags do not need FiR' , you can unlock maps without getting stuck even early on when gear is scarce.
  • If you have Prestiged, also check Prestige requirements and rewards. PVE level 1 has lighter requirements than PVP.
  • In raid, hover over an item and press F2 — Price Overlay instantly shows whether the item is needed for quests or the Hideout and what it sells for.
